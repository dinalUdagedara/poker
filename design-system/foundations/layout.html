Space & elevation

A table is not a dashboard. The scale runs at full density on a 4px base and the radii are generous, because everything in this room — chips, cards, the rail, the plates — is a rounded physical object.

Elevation

On a dark ground a shadow alone is invisible, so elevation here is two things at once: a shadow beneath and a lit hairline along the top edge. The hairline is brass, because every lit edge in this room is brass. That is what makes a panel look milled rather than pasted on.

sm

Resting — tags, chips, a card

md

Raised — plates, the console

lg

Over everything — dialogs, the rail

Motion

Every duration in the system is a physical claim. Dealing is quick and light; chips have weight and overshoot slightly as they land; the pot takes its time crossing the felt, because that journey is the whole point of the animation. All of it drops under prefers-reduced-motion, and anything that ends on nothing is removed rather than frozen.

--dur-deal 260ms --dur-wager 420ms --dur-award 1500ms --ease cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.3,1)
