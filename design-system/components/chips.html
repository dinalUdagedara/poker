Chips

The colours are the casino convention players already read, and they are denominations — never re-tinted to match a screen, never used decoratively. Because they are fixed, a pile in the middle can be weighed against the pile behind a seat without either number being read. That is the whole reason to draw chips at all.

Chips are decorative to a screen reader — the exact figure always sits beside them in .money — so the discs carry aria-hidden and the reader is spared them. Columns sit on a shared baseline, so a short pile reads as less rather than as a stack floating off the felt.
