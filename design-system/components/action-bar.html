Action dock

A sizing row over the three plays, after ClubGG’s client. The pot keys size a real pot raise — measured from the level you would call to, so 100% facing a bet is the call, then the whole pot including it. The amount can be typed into, and the key that commits says the figure on its second line.

Facing a bet

Pot 150, 100 to call, raise from 200. 33% would be 183, under the minimum, so it sits darker; only one key is ever lit.

Checked to you

Nothing to call, so the red key reads Check / Fold and checks: nothing is lost by checking, and only a bet coming back round can fold the hand.

On a desktop

The hand log and the finished hands are grey keys at either end of the dock, so they cost no height of their own. Both open over the table — a sheet on a phone, a dialog on a desktop — never a page that leaves the game. On a phone the slider goes, and the dock stays two rows.
