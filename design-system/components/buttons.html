The three plays

Giving up, staying in, and putting chips in are different decisions and must not look alike at a glance. They are separated by material: fold is unlit stone, the one cold surface in a warm room; check / call is felt green; bet / raise is the only metal on the screen. Full-width and equal, so folding never gets more weight than the bet being sized above it.
