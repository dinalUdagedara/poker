Panels & wells

A panel is a slab of the house resting on the felt: warm, dark, and milled along the top edge. It is never a flat translucent rectangle — over a lit table that reads as a smudge rather than as an object. A well is the opposite gesture: something set into a panel, so it is recessed and unlit.
