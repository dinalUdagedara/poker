Seat

A seat is one object. The hole cards tuck behind the top edge of the plate rather than floating above it, and the plate carries the stack, the face and the name together — otherwise a seat reads as three pieces stacked up. Only the bottom edge of the cards goes under: these carry rank and suit in the middle, so burying more covers the very thing a revealed card is for.

Rules
  • The stack leads and the name is its caption. Whose seat this is gets read once; what they have left is read on every decision, and it was the smaller of the two.
  • Whose turn it is is said on the felt, not only on the plate. A tinted border is the same weight as every other border on a busy table.
  • Winning is the one green thing in the room, and it is the only place green appears outside the pass action.
  • Everything that can appear at a seat — wager, callout, badge — has its height reserved. Seats are centred on their own box, so a growing row nudges the seat.
